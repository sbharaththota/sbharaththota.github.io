About

Beyond the résumé

I'm a Senior Data Engineer based in Dallas–Fort Worth, focused on making complex data systems dependable and useful. My work has centered on the details that make platforms production-ready: how changes arrive, why numbers disagree, what happens when a pipeline fails at 2 a.m., and whether a business team can explain the data it trusts.

I've worked across financial reporting, payment feeds, cloud warehouses, and enterprise integration. I enjoy moving between the architecture level—choosing the right patterns for ingestion, modeling, and governance—and the practical level of SQL plans, PySpark jobs, incident logs, and reliable deployment workflows.

Beyond established data engineering, I'm interested in the intersection of data platforms and AI: retrieval-aware applications, agentic workflows, and tools that help engineers investigate issues faster without compromising correctness or control. I believe strong engineering starts with curiosity, grows through collaboration, and is measured by the impact it delivers.
